Delivery policy

Last updated August 10, 2026

PrivacyTermsRefundsDelivery
Public-preview policy. Secure payment is not active until seller-owned checkout links are connected. This policy should be reviewed again before paid promotion begins.

1. Digital-only delivery

The current products are delivered as digital files. No physical poster, frame, or shipment is included.

2. Estimated timing

The working delivery target is 2–3 business days after payment is confirmed and complete design details are received. The Gift Edition may require the full three-business-day window because it includes alternate captions and a review step.

3. What is required to begin

Production requires a valid order reference and a restorable design link. The design must include the intended place, wording, map view, theme, layout, and format. Missing information can pause the delivery window.

4. Delivery method

Finished files will be delivered electronically, normally to the email address associated with the hosted checkout. The order brief may include an optional contact email for matching and review.

5. File packages

  • Digital Keepsake: a watermark-free high-resolution PNG.
  • Print-Ready Pack: the digital image set plus a print-ready PDF and alternate formats.
  • Gift Edition: the print-ready package plus caption variations and a shareable gift-card image.

6. Delays

Delivery can be delayed by incomplete details, inaccessible design links, payment-review issues, unusually high demand, or external service outages. A revised estimate should be communicated when a material delay is known.

7. File retention

Keep the receipt, order brief, cart backup, and delivered files. The public MVP does not provide a permanent customer account or guaranteed long-term download library.
