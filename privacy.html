Public-preview policy. Secure payment is not active until seller-owned checkout links are connected. This policy should be reviewed again before paid promotion begins.

1. The short version

The current website is deliberately local-first. Your draft map, browser cart, and optional order notes are stored in your browser rather than in a Where It Happened account or database.

2. Information stored on your device

The website may store the following in browser local storage:

  • Your current map design, including coordinates, wording, map position, theme, layout, and format.
  • Cart items and their restorable design links.
  • An optional name, email address, gift recipient, and order note entered in the checkout-review dialog.
  • A selected product and a pending order reference.

This information remains on the device and browser profile where it was created unless you share, export, or submit it elsewhere.

3. Shared design links

When you use “Share design,” the design is encoded into the page URL. Anyone who receives that link can open the design. Do not place private messages or sensitive personal information in a design you intend to share.

4. External map and search services

The website loads map resources from MapLibre and OpenFreeMap and can request notable-place search results from Wikipedia. Those services may receive standard connection information such as your IP address, browser information, and the requested resource. Their own privacy terms apply.

5. Payments

This website does not collect or store card details. Once checkout is activated, payment will occur on a third-party hosted checkout. The payment provider will process the information required to complete the transaction under its own privacy policy.

6. Cart backups and order briefs

A downloaded cart backup or order brief may contain design links, coordinates, order notes, and optional contact details. Store those files privately and delete them when they are no longer needed.

7. Clearing local information

You can clear the cart from the cart drawer. You can also remove this website’s stored data through your browser settings. Clearing browser storage may permanently remove drafts and cart items from that device.

8. Contact and policy changes

A dedicated customer-support address will be published before live checkout is enabled. Material policy changes will be reflected by updating the date shown on this page.
