Questions before buying

The practical details.

Is this a physical poster?

No. The current products are digital file packages. Physical printing and shipping are not included yet.

Can I edit a design after adding it to the cart?

Yes. Open the cart, choose “Edit design,” make the changes, and add the updated version again.

Are the free downloads usable?

Yes, for evaluation and sharing. Free PNG and print previews include a visible preview watermark.

When will paid files arrive?

The working delivery target is 2–3 business days after payment and complete design details are received.

What happens if checkout is not connected?

You can still save the cart, copy the complete order brief, or download an order request. No payment is collected in that mode.
