Refund policy

Last updated August 10, 2026

PrivacyTermsRefundsDelivery
Public-preview policy. Secure payment is not active until seller-owned checkout links are connected. This policy should be reviewed again before paid promotion begins.

1. Current preview

No payment is collected while checkout links are blank. Downloading an order request does not create a paid order and does not charge you.

2. Cancellation before work begins

After live checkout is activated, a paid order may be cancelled for a full refund when the request is received before personalized production work has begun.

3. After personalized work begins

Because each product is made from a customer-specific design, orders are generally non-refundable after personalized production has begun. This does not remove the right to receive the product that was ordered or to have delivery errors corrected.

4. Incorrect or defective delivery

If the delivered file does not match the accepted design link, is corrupted, or omits a file included in the purchased package, the issue will be corrected without an additional product charge. Report the problem within 14 days of delivery and include the order reference.

5. Customer-supplied errors

Spelling, date, coordinate, and wording errors that were present in the submitted design are not considered product defects. A reasonable correction may still be offered, but substantial redesign can require a new order.

6. Printing results

Screen colors, home printers, local print shops, paper, trimming, and framing can produce different results. Refunds are not normally available for variations caused by equipment or materials outside the delivered digital file.

7. Final review before activation

This is the proposed launch policy. The support address, request process, and any jurisdiction-specific rights will be confirmed before real payments are enabled.
